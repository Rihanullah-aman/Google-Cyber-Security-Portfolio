# Security Risk Assessment Report — Hardening Recommendations

## Overview
Recommends three hardening tools/methods to reduce security risk on a network, with reasoning for each.

## Recommended hardening measures

1. **Password policies**
   Enforce minimum length, an approved character set, and a no-sharing rule. Lock accounts out after five consecutive failed login attempts to limit brute force risk.

2. **Firewall maintenance**
   Regularly review and update firewall configurations and rules, especially in response to new security issues or suspicious network traffic.

3. **Multifactor authentication (MFA)**
   Require at least two distinct verification factors before granting access, making it significantly harder for attackers to break in even with a compromised password — and discouraging password sharing among staff, particularly those with admin access.

## Takeaway
Combining strong password policies, up-to-date firewall rules, and MFA addresses both external attack vectors (brute forcing, network intrusion) and internal risk (credential sharing), giving layered protection rather than relying on a single control.
